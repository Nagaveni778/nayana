Famous Leaders of India

Mahatma Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi was a great leader of India's freedom movement and promoted truth and non-violence.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar was a social reformer, jurist and an important architect of the Indian Constitution.

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel played an important role in India's freedom movement and national integration.

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was a renowned scientist and former President of India.

Subhas Chandra Bose

Subhas Chandra Bose was a prominent freedom fighter and leader in India's independence struggle.

Jawaharlal Nehru

Jawaharlal Nehru was India's first Prime Minister and contributed to the development of modern India.

Bhagat Singh

Bhagat Singh was a courageous revolutionary and freedom fighter who inspired generations of Indians.

Savitribai Phule

Savitribai Phule was a pioneering educator and social reformer who worked for women's education and equality.

Dr. Rajendra Prasad

Dr. Rajendra Prasad was India's first President and an important leader of the freedom movement.

Rani Lakshmibai

Rani Lakshmibai was a courageous queen of Jhansi and an important figure in the 1857 uprising.
